Description:

This repository contains an analysis of a patient dataset investigating the relationship between education and symptoms associated with Alzheimer's disease.

The project explores whether educational background is correlated with the onset or severity of Alzheimer's-related symptoms. The analysis uses patient-level data to identify patterns and relationships between education and variables such as age of onset of symptoms or age of death. 

Research Questions:

Is there a correlation between education and age of onset of dementia?
Is there a correlation between level of education and age of death in Alzheimer's patients?
Does the number of years of education impact whether or not a patient develops dementia?
Is there a relationship between the highest level of education and the last recorded mmse score?

Analysis of Data:

The analysis of the data was conducted using tools such as pandas and matplotlib. The data set was sorted and relevant variables were compared using scatterplots or bar graphs. A statistical analysis of these results was also conducted using linear regression functions, t-tests, and an ANOVA. These values were compared to the alpha value to determine statistical significance. 

Results and Conclusions:

The R^2 values of the scatterplots were 0.03 and 0.00 respectively. The p-values of the bar graphs were .0507 and .773. The p-value of the datasets were both greater than the alpha value of .05, and the R^2 values of the scatterplots were close to 0 which indicates no statistically significant difference. Further statistical analysis is included in the repository.
